About

Helm listens on the page you're viewing — when it hears the wake word, it sends what you said (plus a short description of clickable things on the page) to Anthropic's API. Voice transcription runs locally in Chrome. Nothing is stored on a server.

Toggle the extension on/off any time from its icon. To unload entirely, remove it from chrome://extensions. Your settings and API key live in this browser's local storage.
